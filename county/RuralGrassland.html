Air pollutant sequestration by grasslands in rural areas

PM2.5 PM10 NO2 SO2

Grasslands help remove air pollutants by absorbing gases through leaves and intercepting particulate matter. These nature-based solutions improve air quality and reduce health risks. The ecosystem services provided by grasslands highlight the importance of preserving these landscapes for air quality regulation.

The data in this work is based on modeled estimates of air pollutant sequestration by grasslands in rural areas in each county, measured in grams per unit area of grassland. Methods and data sources are detailed in the study below, published by researchers from The Ohio State University, The Davey Institute, the University of Leeds, and the U.S. Forest Service.
